How to play

  • Hold to steer right. Let go to drift left.
  • Don't touch the pins or the stitched edges.
  • Pass close to pins for points. The closer, the more.
  • ×
    Close calls grow your multiplier and slow you down a little. Play it safe and the multiplier drains.

    Grab squares for double points. They speed you up a little.
