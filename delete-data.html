Delete your data

Last updated 6 November 2026

Fastest: from inside the game

  1. Open byathread.app and sign in with the same Google or Facebook account.
  2. Tap Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account, then tap again to confirm.

This erases your account, display name, every score you've posted, your friend code and your friends list at once. It can't be undone.

By email

If you can't sign in anymore, email hello@byathread.app from the address of the Google or Facebook account you used. Tell us which one you used. We'll delete everything within 30 days and confirm by email.

Facebook users

You can also remove By a Thread under Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites. That stops Facebook sharing data with us. Use one of the options above to delete what we already store.

Data on your device

Scores and settings saved without an account live only in your browser. Clearing this site's data in your browser settings removes them. You can switch off the anonymous gameplay stats in Profile. Those stats aren't linked to you and are deleted automatically after 13 months.
